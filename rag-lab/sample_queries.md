# Sample Queries

Use these examples to smoke-test retrieval quality in a RAG demo.

## Patient questions

- Do you take my insurance, and how much will a crown cost?
- My temporary crown came off after dinner. What should I do tonight?
- Can I bring my child for their first visit even if they are nervous?
- I am pregnant. Is it safe to get dental X-rays or a cleaning?
- My gums bleed when I floss. Should I stop flossing?
- How do I reschedule if I cannot make tomorrow morning's appointment?
- Do you offer whitening, and will it work on old fillings?
- I woke up with swelling near a back tooth. Can I wait until next week?
- What should I expect after a tooth extraction?
- Can I get antibiotics over the phone for tooth pain?

## Staff and operations questions

- How should the front desk handle a same-day emergency call?
- What should we tell a patient who is upset about an insurance estimate?
- What documents does a new patient need before the first appointment?
- How do we explain periodontal maintenance versus a regular cleaning?
- What is our cancellation policy for repeated no-shows?
- What should assistants check before seating a crown delivery patient?
- Which home-care instructions should be sent after scaling and root planing?
- How do we document a patient who declines X-rays?
